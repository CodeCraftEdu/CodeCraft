## Prompt complet de production

Create an ORIGINAL test illustration for CodeCraft, inspired by the attached PS1 screenshot's visual language, NOT a copy of its level or architecture. Landscape 1536x1024, 3:2. Looks like an actual late-1990s PlayStation fantasy platform game screenshot: very few broad polygonal volumes, tiny soft painted textures, modest vertex shading, imperfect chunky silhouettes. No modern low-poly diorama lighting, no realistic materials, no depth of field, no tessellated multicolor triangles.

ENVIRONMENT: a simple wide turquoise grassy platform with dark muted violet sides, one low raised terrace behind it, just three broad pale lavender angular rock forms on the horizon, and a single small squat circular pavilion with a plain arched opening off to one side. Sparse, open and minimal, most of ground unobstructed. Do NOT reproduce the reference castle, crystal towers, pink lava, bridge layout, or constellation of islands. No gems, collectibles or detailed ornament.

SKYBOX is important: takes up almost HALF the image, painted dark plum/indigo gradient with two broad hazy diagonal mauve and muted peach cloud brushstrokes, low contrast and few tiny faint stars. Emulate the simple painted PS1 skybox of the reference, not a galaxy photo, not elaborate nebula filaments. Terrain remains clearly lit with broad saturated turquoise, lavender and soft warm accents.

TWO ENTIRELY NEW ORIGINAL NONHUMAN FANTASY CREATURES in foreground, very different silhouettes from stocky humanoids:

- left: a squat dusty pink rounded pear-bodied hopping creature, enormous broad flattened three-toed feet, tiny forelimbs, a short broad tail, almost no separate neck, blunt oval head merging into body.
- right: a small pale teal low quadruped with a wide rounded rectangular body, four stubby flipper-like feet, long simple curved tail and an oval blunt head.
  Both are friendly abstract fantasy beasts, visibly angular softly shaded PS1 volumes, not real animals or recognizable existing characters. NO HORNS. NO FACIAL FEATURES: no eyes, mouth, nostrils, eyebrows or drawn face; keep only the head's simple irregular oval volume, not a perfect smooth sphere. No human clothing, shoes, human hair, jewelry, props or weapons. Entire bodies visible, not oversized, with negative space around them. No third character, HUD, text, logo or watermark. Preserve simplicity over adding detail.
